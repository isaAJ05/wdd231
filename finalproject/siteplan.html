BookVerse

BookVerse combines the words Book and Universe, representing a place where readers can explore an endless collection of books, discover new stories, and connect with their reading interests.

Site Purporse

BookVerse is a web application designed to help users discover, organize, and explore books. Visitors can search for books by title, author, or genre, view detailed information, browse popular and trending books, and save their favorite titles for future reading. The website aims to make finding the next great book easier and more enjoyable for readers of all ages.

Scenarios

What are the most popular books in my favorite genre right now?

How can I save books that I want to read later?
